\small

\toprule
\textbf{Category} & \textbf{Criteria} & \textbf{Interpretation} \\
\midrule
\textit{Validly Safe} &
Low Conditional ASR, high VRR, high VSR, and preserved utility &
The model generates valid responses while maintaining both safety and task performance. \\
\addlinespace

\textit{False Safe} &
Low Original ASR accompanied by low VRR or abnormal PPL &
The model appears safe because of invalid responses or generation failures rather than meaningful safety preservation. \\
\addlinespace

\textit{False Unsafe} &
High Original ASR accompanied by a large proportion of invalid responses &
Invalid responses may be incorrectly classified as successful attacks by the evaluator. \\
\addlinespace

\textit{Unsafe but Functional} &
High VRR together with high Conditional ASR &
The model continues to generate valid responses, but frequently complies with harmful requests. \\
\bottomrule
